# Split Editor

The split editor shows a second note to the right of the open one, so you can read one note while writing another, or compare two.

## Opening a split

- Press `Ctrl+\` (**Split Editor**) to show the open note in a second pane on the right.
- Or right-click any tab and choose to open it in a split on the right.

The right-hand pane has a small header with the note's path and a **×** to close it. `Ctrl+Shift+\` (**Close Split**) closes it too.

## How it works

- There is one tab bar. The left pane shows the active tab; the right pane shows the note you split off. Clicking another tab changes the left pane only.
- Both panes are full editors: you can type in either, and each saves on its own. The right pane follows that note's own view mode (Live, Source or View).
- Drag the divider between the panes to resize them; neither side gets narrower than 200 pixels.
- The split is not kept when the page is reloaded.

## Ideas

- **Reference and draft** — an outline or source material on the right, your writing on the left.
- **Compare versions** — a conflict copy beside the original (the [conflict banner](../features/conflicts.md) also has its own **Compare** view).
- **Read and write** — put a note in View mode on the right while you edit on the left.

On a phone, the screen is too narrow for two panes; open the notes in turn instead.
