# Attachments and Trash

## Attachments

Images, PDFs, audio, video and other files that your notes use are stored with the vault as **attachments**.

Open the attachment manager from the ribbon: **More tools (⋯) → Attachments**. There you can:

- **add files** — drag and drop them onto the drop zone, or click it to choose files (up to 25 MB each);
- **insert** one into the open note — click it; Noteriv adds the Markdown link at the cursor (`![](…)` for images);
- **filter** by type (images, audio, video, PDF, other) and sort by name or date;
- **delete** one (this cannot be undone).

Attachments also arrive from [importing](./import-export.md) a folder or zip, from the audio recorder (a [core plugin](./core-plugins.md), off by default), and from [sync](../sync/index.md).

Images display inline in Live and View mode; audio links get a player; PDFs open in the [PDF viewer](../views/pdf-viewer.md).

Each vault has room for **500 MB** of attachments.

Adding attachments needs a verified email address. See [Before your email is verified](../getting-started/account.md#before-your-email-is-verified).

## Trash

Deleting a note or folder (sidebar right-click → **Delete**) moves it to the **trash**; nothing is removed at once.

Open it from the ribbon: **More tools (⋯) → Trash**, or **File → Trash**. For each item:

- **Restore file** — puts it back where it was;
- **Delete permanently** — removes it for good.

**Empty Trash** deletes everything in it. Items left in the trash are deleted automatically after **30 days**; the trash shows how many days each one has left.

Conflict copies you discard from the [conflict banner](./conflicts.md) go to the trash too.

> Deleting a whole **vault** (from the vault switcher) does not use the trash: it is permanent. Export a zip first if you may need the notes again.
