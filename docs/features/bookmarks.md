# Bookmarks

Bookmarks keep the notes you use most one click away, wherever they sit in the folder tree.

## Adding and removing

- **Bookmark this note** — the bookmark icon in the open note's toolbar. Click it again (**Remove bookmark**) to remove the bookmark.
- `Ctrl+Shift+B` (`Cmd+Shift+B` on a Mac) toggles the bookmark of the open note.
- In the Bookmarks panel, each entry has a remove button.

## The Bookmarks panel

Open it with **Bookmarks** in the ribbon. It opens on the right of the editor and lists your bookmarked notes, with their folder when they are not at the root. The note you have open is highlighted. Click an entry to open it.

With nothing bookmarked yet, the panel says so and reminds you of the bookmark icon in the note toolbar.

## Where bookmarks are kept

Bookmarks belong to the vault and are stored with it on the server, so they are the same on every device. They point at the note itself, not at its path: renaming or moving a bookmarked note keeps the bookmark.

## Tips

- Bookmark hub notes — an index, a project overview, a reading list — rather than every note.
- Combine bookmarks with [Quick Open](./search.md) (`Ctrl+K`) for everything else.
