# Language

Noteriv's interface is available in **English** and **Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt)**.

## How the language is chosen

1. **Your choice**, if you made one — in **Settings → General → Language**, or with **English · Tiếng Việt** at the bottom of the sign-in and sign-up pages.
2. Otherwise **your browser's language**: Vietnamese if your browser prefers it, English for anything else.

The choice belongs to your account, so it follows you to every device you sign in on. Choose **Follow the browser** to go back to the automatic choice.

The interface switches at once; no reload is needed. Core plugins (calendar, templates, flashcards…) switch with it.

## What changes and what does not

- **Changes:** menus, buttons, settings, messages, core plugin labels, date formats, and the names given to new notes and folders ("Untitled" / "Chưa đặt tên", "New Folder" / "Thư mục mới").
- **Does not change:** your notes, their names, and the names of folders you made. The **Getting started** vault is written in the language you signed up in ("Getting started" or "Bắt đầu").
- **Legal pages** (Terms of Use, Privacy Policy) show both languages, English first.

> **Desktop app:** **Settings → General → Language** offers "Follow the system" instead of the browser.
