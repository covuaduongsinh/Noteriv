# Signing Up and Installing

Noteriv runs in any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer, phone or tablet. There is nothing to download: open your Noteriv address and sign in. You can also install it so it opens like an app.

## Create an account

1. Open your Noteriv address and choose **Sign up** on the sign-in page.
2. If the page asks for an **Invite code**, sign-up on this server is by invitation: enter the code you were given. Otherwise anyone can sign up.
3. Enter a display name, your email address and a password (at least 8 characters), accept the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy, and choose **Sign up**. Some servers show a short check that you are not a bot (Cloudflare Turnstile) above the button; it usually passes by itself.

Your account opens on **Your vaults**, with a **Getting started** vault already there. The [quickstart](quickstart.md) takes it from here.

Noteriv also emails you a link to verify your address. If you forget your password later, **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page sends a reset link. See [Your account](account.md).

The sign-in and sign-up pages offer **English · Tiếng Việt** at the bottom; the language you choose there becomes your account's language. See [Language](../features/language.md).

## Install it like an app

Installed, Noteriv opens full screen from its own icon, and a long press on the icon offers **Quick note**. The page **Install on your phone** (linked from the vault list and the sign-in page, at `/install`) walks you through it; in short:

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open your Noteriv address in Chrome and sign in.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Confirm. Noteriv appears on your home screen.

### iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open your Noteriv address in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Add**.

### Computer (Chrome or Edge)

Use the install icon at the end of the address bar, or the **Install the app** button on the `/install` page. Noteriv then opens in its own window and appears among your applications.

An installed Noteriv opens even without a connection; vaults and notes you opened recently can be read and edited, and your edits are sent when you are back online. See [Offline use](../features/offline.md) and [Phone and tablet](../mobile/index.md).

## Updates

The web app updates itself: a new version is used from the next time you open or reload the page. There is nothing to install.

## Desktop app

There is also a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux, in which a vault is a folder of Markdown files on your computer. It is built from this repository; see [Desktop app](../desktop.md).
